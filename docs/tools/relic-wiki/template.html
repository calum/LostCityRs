Overview

  • Finish any of the {{NTASKS}} tasks for a pick of up to 3 random relics. There are more tasks than relics, so choose your own path.
  • Kill the King Black Dragon and the Kalphite Queen (tasks 24 and 25) to finish the run. They give no relic.
  • You start with 10x XP and unlimited run energy. The XP Multiplier relic triples your rate each time you take it: 30x, 90x, 270x.
  • Failed skilling attempts get a second 50% roll. Combat is not affected.
  • Your 10th task always gives Quest Pass. Every 5th task offers an XP Multiplier.
  • Combat relics 3 to 9 each add +50% accuracy and stack.
  • With friends, everyone who hits a boss before it dies gets the kill.
  • Game Options tab (wrench): Tasks and Relics buttons show your progress.
